🦊 Maginvox

A quiet world, waiting to be discovered.

About

Maginvox is a game about exploring and gathering information about the world. You play as a little fox character who wanders through the woods, uncovering stories and secrets along the way.

Features

  • Play as a little fox exploring the woods
  • Focus on exploration and learning, not fighting
  • Very little combat
  • Relaxing, calm music throughout

Who it's for

If you enjoy slow, thoughtful games about discovery rather than combat, Maginvox is a forest worth exploring.
